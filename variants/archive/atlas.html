II About
The longer story

I write data pipelines for a living and pretend I'm setting type for a periodical.

By trade I'm an SRE at Barclays in Knutsford, building observability for Infrastructure — Kafka, OpenTelemetry, Elastic, real-time alarm shipping. Before that I spent four years on Data Integration in Pune, automating the unromantic parts of the job.

Before that I co-founded two small studios — Infin8y Labs (fandom posters with a friend) and The Jellyfish Collective (branding for early-stage startups, Paris-remote).

I have a B.E. in IT (top 3% of department) and an M.A. in Economics (8.88 CGPA, Fergusson). I keep a Now page, take photos, write essays, and think a lot about justice as a measurable concept.
