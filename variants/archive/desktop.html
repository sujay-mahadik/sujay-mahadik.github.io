hi, i'm sujay.

Developer · Designer · Dreamer

An engineer who keeps a design studio in his pocket, and an economist who reads philosophy in the margins. This portfolio is a desktop, not a feed — drag the icons, open the windows, leave the sticky notes where you want them.

By trade I'm an SRE at Barclays in Knutsford, building observability for Infrastructure. By temperament I'm a designer who co-founded two small studios in Pune. By degree I have an M.A. in Economics and a soft spot for justice as a measurable concept.

about.me

about, the long version

I write data pipelines for a living and pretend I'm setting type for a periodical. The two crafts are closer than they look — both are about deciding what counts as a unit, where the gutters go, and which detail rewards a closer look.

I grew up in Pune, studied IT at MIT College of Engineering, then doubled back for a Master's in Economics at Fergusson because I wanted a vocabulary for the things engineering kept eliding.

Since 2019 I've been at Barclays — first as a Graduate Analyst, then on Service Delivery for Enterprise Data Integration, and now on the SRE Core team in Knutsford. On the side: Infin8y Labs, The Jellyfish Collective, photography, and the occasional essay on green software and the ethics of measurement.
